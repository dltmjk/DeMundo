# Glossary of renderings — De mundo dialogi tres (Thomas White, 1642)

Fixed English renderings used throughout. Read at the start of each run; add new terms at the end of each run.

## Conventions
- Transcription: long s → s; æ/œ → ae/oe; macrons and q; expanded; consonantal u normalised to v, i kept (Iacobi, Iove); [Margin: …]; {?word} uncertain; {illeg.} illegible.
- English: literal, Latin word order where English allows; supplied words in [square brackets]; Latin of technical/ambiguous words in [brackets] after the English.
